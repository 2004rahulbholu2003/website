# Somewhere Between Destiny & Us ♡
### A Personalized Romantic Birthday Website for My Favourite Person

> *"Ek kahaani hai... jo shayad humne plan nahi ki thi, but somehow, it became my favourite."*

A digital love story built with **React**, **Vite**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **Framer Motion**. Designed as a combination of a romantic storybook, vintage digital diary, cinematic experience, interactive photo album, and handwritten love letter.

## 🎵 Background Music & Autoplay

- When she clicks **"Enter our little universe ♡"** on the welcome screen, the music starts **immediately and automatically**.
- The music continues playing in the background with `loop={true}` as long as she is visiting the website.
- She can use the floating music player at the bottom right to pause, play, mute, or adjust volume anytime.


3. Drag and drop the **`dist`** folder onto the Netlify webpage.
